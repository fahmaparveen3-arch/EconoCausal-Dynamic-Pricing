# EconoCausal — Dataset Research

## Project Objective
Identify a suitable dataset for developing a Dynamic Pricing and Causal AI platform using Double Machine Learning (DML).

## Dataset Candidate
**Hillstrom Email Marketing Dataset**

Source: https://blog.minethatdata.com/2008/03/minethatdata-e-mail-analytics-and-data.html

## Why This Dataset?
- Contains customer marketing-treatment groups.
- Includes customer purchasing outcomes.
- Can support initial exploration of causal inference and uplift modelling.

## Limitations
The dataset focuses on email marketing interventions rather than discount amounts. Further research is required to find discount-specific data or determine whether a synthetic dataset is appropriate for the dynamic pricing use case.

## Planned Data Exploration
1. Inspect columns and data types.
2. Check missing values and duplicate records.
3. Examine treatment-group distribution.
4. Compare spending and conversion outcomes.
5. Assess suitability for causal modelling.

## Current Status
Initial dataset research documented. Dataset validation and final selection are pending.
